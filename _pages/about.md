Hello, and welcome to my website.

I am Kris, currently a postdoctoral researcher working with Ken Miller at the Center for Theoretical Neuroscience, Zuckerman Institute, Columbia University. Previously, I completed my PhD in the lab of Julijana Gjorgjieva at the Max Planck Institute for Brain Research and the Technical University of Munich in Germany.
